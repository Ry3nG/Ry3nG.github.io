Endogenous and Exogenous Memory for World Action Models↗
Preprint, 2026
Zerui Gong*, Marina Barannikov*, Zhonghua Wu*†, Shanshan Lu, Junjie Gao, Zhe Yang, Jinglin He, Quanzhou Li, Weijie Qiao, Qinyue Li, Qingyi Tao, Chen Change Loy

Gives a world action model two memories driven by its video expert, with no extra supervision: endogenous memory tracks task progress, and exogenous memory records where the scene departs from the model’s prediction. Introduces RMBench-Dynamic; 58.8% there, 81.7% on RMBench, and 57 of 60 real-robot trials.

World Models as Native Latent Action Models↗
arXiv, 2026
Qinyue Li*, Weijie Qiao*, Qingyi Tao*†, Zerui Gong, Zhe Yang, Quanzhou Li, Taolin Zhang, Jinglin He, Zhonghua Wu, Chen Change Loy

Uses one pretrained video diffusion transformer as both inverse and forward dynamics model, with learnable dynamics registers as the only bridge, so a video’s motion is distilled into a compact latent action. The latents replay across robots, scenes and from human hands to robots, and teach PAWlicy, a VLA pretraining recipe that reaches 93.6% on RoboTwin 2.0.

Has GPT-6-Astra Achieved Embodied Intelligence?↗
Technical Report, 2026
SenseNova Joint Research Team

A study of how far GPT-6-Astra goes as an embodied agent: 12 benchmarks across manipulation, humanoids, mobile platforms and dexterous hands, a System 2 reasoner with System 1 controller harness, and deployment on eight real robot configurations. It has largely crossed the threshold of embodied competence, but not yet of practical embodied intelligence.

Mundo: Customized World Model↗
Preprint, 2026
Yihang Luo, Zunnan Xu, Shangchen Zhou, Yushi Lan, Kang Liao, Zerui Gong, Xiaoming Wu, Qingyi Tao, Zhonghua Wu, Bixuan Jin, Fei Wang, Zhi Hou, Xiaogang Wang, Xingang Pan, Chen Change Loy

Builds a simulatable world from a few images of a place: the scene is reconstructed as a 3D proxy refined by a VLM agent, a scene program and simulator compute how objects move, and a video model renders the result with the input images as context. The world can be explored from new viewpoints, edited, and used for robot tasks.

Knowledge Visualization: A Benchmark and Method for Knowledge-Intensive Text-to-Image Generation↗
arXiv, 2026
Ran Zhao, Sheng Jin, Size Wu, Kang Liao, Zerui Gong, Zujin Guo, Yang Xiao, Wei Li

KVBench: 1,800 expert-curated prompts from over 30 high-school textbooks in six subjects, on which 14 text-to-image models show clear gaps in scientific correctness. KE-Check enriches prompts with domain knowledge, then edits images against a checklist of constraints, narrowing the gap between open and closed models.

SA-LUT: Spatial Adaptive 4D Look-Up Table for Photorealistic Style Transfer↗
ICCV, 2025
Zerui Gong, Zhonghua Wu, Qingyi Tao, Qinyue Li, Chen Change Loy

Combines LUT efficiency with neural adaptability: a style-guided 4D LUT plus a context map from content-style cross-attention gives spatially adaptive colour transfer that preserves structure. Introduces the PST50 benchmark; 66.7% lower LPIPS than 3D LUT methods, at 16 FPS on video.

OpenUni: A Simple Baseline for Unified Multimodal Understanding and Generation↗
arXiv, 2025
Size Wu, Zhonghua Wu, Zerui Gong, Qingyi Tao, Sheng Jin, Qinyue Li, Wei Li, Chen Change Loy

Bridges an off-the-shelf multimodal LLM and a diffusion model with learnable queries and a light transformer connector. Strong results on GenEval, DPG-Bench and WISE with only 1.1B and 3.1B activated parameters; all weights, code and 23M training image-text pairs are released.
